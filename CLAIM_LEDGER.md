## Governing non-inferences
Aqueous/iontronic memristive devices do not establish generic “water memory.” Molecular polarity does not establish symbolic storage. Device hysteresis may arise from ions, interfaces, geometry, electrochemistry and electrodes; mechanism must be identified. Literature on a related device does not validate this wheel automatically.
