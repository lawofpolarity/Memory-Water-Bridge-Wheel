## What remains scientifically useful

The repository asks a legitimate research question once the stronger historical claims are removed:

> Can a controlled aqueous or iontronic physical system exhibit reproducible, history-dependent electrical state that is useful as a computational memory or reservoir?

That question is experimentally testable. It is different from claiming that bulk water possesses persistent symbolic, holographic, biological or recursive memory.

Relevant physical mechanisms must be stated specifically—e.g. ionic transport, electrode polarization, electrochemical state, geometry-dependent hysteresis or fluidic nanochannel dynamics—rather than being attributed generically to “water memory.”
